MEDRESCUE

Stage 1: Emergency Ward. Pick the right treatment before the patient's clock runs out.

How to play

  • Ten patients arrive one at a time. Read the symptoms and vitals.
  • Choose the correct first-aid treatment (click, tap, or press 1–4).
  • You have 15 seconds per patient. Faster answers earn more points.
  • Wrong answers and timeouts cost a heart. Lose all 3 and the stage ends.
  • Correct answers in a row build a combo multiplier up to ×3.
